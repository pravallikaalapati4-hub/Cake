Our Story

The journey of bringing the fizz into every bite

Coke Bake was born from a simple idea: what if the iconic refreshment of Coca-Cola could live inside a dessert? We set out to craft a premium bakery experience that celebrates that unmistakable cola character — sweet, slightly spicy, and endlessly nostalgic — in cupcakes, cakes, pastries and cream desserts.
